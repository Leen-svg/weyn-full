About us

The question we all
grew up asking. وين؟

Weyn means "where"

It's the first word of every plan in this part of the world. وين نروح؟ Where are we going? We've heard it a thousand times in our own group chats, and watched a thousand plans die right after it.

So we thought the question deserved a real answer. Not a list of fifty places. Not another map of identical star ratings. Three good ones, picked for you and whoever you're going with.

What we're building

Weyn is an app you tap, not one you type into. You tell it how you're feeling, it gives you three real places, and your group votes on the link. Around that: friends, a feed of where your people actually go, saved places, ratings, and points for trying somewhere new.

It's live and free right now. A live map of the city, and picks that learn your taste on their own, are the two things we're building next.

We're live across Abu Dhabi and Dubai, building carefully from our home in the UAE.

Why it matters to us

This comes from things we've actually lived. The cousins we keep meaning to call. The friends twenty minutes away we haven't seen in years. We want people to live again, to be social again, and for going out to feel fun instead of admin.

See you out there.
