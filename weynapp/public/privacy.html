Privacy

Your data.
Plain language.

This is a plain-language draft for the beta period. A formal privacy policy ships before we launch properly.

What we collect today

  • Update list: your email address, so we can tell you the day it hits the App Store and Google Play. That's it.
  • The beta: you can use it with no account at all. If you make one, we store your email, your display name and photo if you add them, the places you save and rate, your friends, and your points. Voting on a poll someone sent you stores nothing but the vote.

What we'll never do

  • Sell your data. To anyone. Ever.
  • Share your location without you turning it on. The future live map is friends-only and opt-in, and you choose who sees you and when.

Leaving

Want off the list? Reply "remove me" to any of our emails, or write to hello@goweyn.com, and you're gone. No questions, no guilt trip.
