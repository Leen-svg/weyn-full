From the Weyn team

Why we're actually building this

Weyn started in our own dead group chats. The cousin we keep meaning to call. The uni friend who lives twenty minutes away and we haven't seen in three years. Every "we should hang out soon" that never turned into a day, a time and a place.

So this one's for them. For your family. For the friend you've been missing without noticing. For the version of you that loves people but is tired of always being the one who plans.

All of it comes from things we've actually lived. We're not trying to build the next big thing. We want people to live again. To be social again. For going out to feel fun instead of admin.

Now live across Abu Dhabi and Dubai, built in the UAE. See you out there.
