Roadmap

Where this is going.
Literally.

Now

The beta, live and free

Up right now at goweyn.com/app. Tap how you're feeling, get three spots, send them to your group as a poll, save places into your own lists, and turn them into a plan. The curated catalogue covers both Abu Dhabi and Dubai and changes as the beta grows.

Next

Picks that learn you

Right now it goes off what you tap. Next it goes off what you actually do: where you went, what you rated, who you went with, what time you like being out. The more you use it, the less you'll have to tell it.

Then

The live map

The city, live. What's buzzing right now, vibes posted by people who are actually there, and which of your friends are out tonight. Friends only, opt in, always.

Later

Then the App Store, then every Middle Eastern city

Dubai's already starting to fill in. Then Riyadh, Doha, Jeddah, Amman, Beirut and beyond. You decide the order, so tell us your city and shout loudly.
